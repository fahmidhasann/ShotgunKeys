Drop your own custom audio files (.wav or .mp3) here to customize ShotgunKeys!

File naming conventions:
- For Shotgun Blast: name files starting with "blast" (e.g. blast1.wav, blast2.wav, blast.mp3) or "shotgun_blast"
- For Shotgun Reload: name files starting with "reload" (e.g. reload1.wav, reload.wav, reload.mp3) or "shotgun_reload"

After adding files, click "Reload Audio Files" in the ShotgunKeys Menu Bar popup!
